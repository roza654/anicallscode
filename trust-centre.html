Data Use

How we use your information

Your information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare workforce recommendations, confirm bookings, manage your account where applicable, and meet our own record-keeping obligations. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies & Storage

What we store in your browser

This site does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. A small amount of browser local storage is used for functional purposes only, for example remembering which office you last selected on the Contact page. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings without affecting how the site works.

Data Protection

How we protect your information

Every form on this site is built around the same set of protections, regardless of which team receives your submission.

✓Encrypted in transit

Every form submission and account action runs over HTTPS.

✓Rate limiting

Automated checks block repeat and duplicate submissions from abusing our forms.

✓Hashed passwords

Account passwords are stored as salted hashes. We never store or see them in plain text.

✓Secure file handling

Uploaded files are restricted by type and size, and are never executed as code.

✓Access controls

Stored data is only accessible to the team members who need it to do their job.

✓Data minimisation

We ask for what a form needs to do its job, and nothing beyond that.

✓Audit logging

Errors are logged in detail for our engineers. You are only ever shown a plain, honest message.

✓No ad trackers

No third-party advertising or analytics trackers run on this site.

Your Rights

Your rights over your information

Depending on where you are based, data protection law gives you specific rights over the information we hold about you. This includes the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we are not required to keep it
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information
  • Request a copy of your information in a portable format
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sales@anicalls.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.
