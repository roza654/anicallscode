This page prices IT-enabled services. Specialist roles are a different book.

Two rate books, and they are not interchangeable

IT-enabled services (this page): service desk, technical support, application support, back-office processing, finance and accounting operations, data operations, customer operations. Repeatable, volume-driven, delivered to a defined scope and SLA.

Specialist and enterprise consulting (priced separately): SAP S/4HANA, Oracle Fusion, core banking, cybersecurity architecture, data engineering, cloud architecture. Scarce, certification-gated, priced on scarcity rather than cost-plus.

The rates below run roughly 60% to 75% below our specialist rates for a reason: they are a different labour market. Applying an ITES rate to an S/4HANA architect would not be a discount, it would be a misquote. Ask us for the specialist rate card if that is what you need.

Why we publish line 03

Most offshore vendors quote you a single number and let you assume it is mostly salary. It is not. Running a service desk means paying for supervision, quality checking, holiday cover and the licences the desk runs on, whether or not anyone mentions them. We would rather show you that cost than pretend our margin is 75%.

Why the gates exist, stated plainly

A 15% margin on delivered cost is a 13% gross margin. That covers the sales cost, the corporate overhead of operating in six countries, and a profit, only when it is spread across a large contract. Why South Africa and Malaysia cost more than India, and when to pay it

South Africa runs UTC+2 with no daylight saving, giving six or more hours of live overlap with a European working day and nobody on a night shift. Malaysia runs UTC+8, matching Singapore, Hong Kong and Perth, with English, Bahasa, Mandarin and Tamil coverage. If your scope is asynchronous and English-only, India is cheaper and deeper and we will tell you so. If your users need someone reachable during their own afternoon, the difference buys exactly that.

An honest word about the onshore rates

UK contract IT day rates fell year on year through 2026, and our onshore rates reflect that market rather than the one before it. But onshore ITES is thin for every supplier including us, and there is very little room left in these numbers. If your budget sits below them, the answer is not a cheaper onshore contractor — it is moving the queue offshore. We will show you which tickets can move and which genuinely cannot.

The honest case

Why this pricing, and where it does not suit you

What you get for the margin

Cost-plus only works if you trust the cost. Ours is built from published market salary data, refreshed quarterly, and we will show you the source for any line in the table on request. If the market moves down, your rate moves down at the next review. That cuts both ways and we have written it that way deliberately.

Four things that come with the rate

One contract across six countries. Most organisations solving a multi-country support problem end up with a vendor per market and nobody accountable for the outcome. · A published transfer price on every BOT. Named at signature, not negotiated when you want to leave. · Local-content compliance where it matters. B-BBEE Level 2 in South Africa, Emiratisation-eligible placements in the UAE, Bumiputera participation in Malaysia. · Governed AI inside the desk. Deflection and triage run through Agent OS with a named human reviewing consequential output, which is what lets us hold these rates as volumes grow.

Where we are not the right answer

If you want the single cheapest quote for a small offshore desk, a single-country regional provider will beat us — they carry one entity and one overhead, and we carry six. If you need specialist enterprise consulting rather than IT-enabled services, this page is the wrong rate card and the right one costs several times more. And if you need a globally recognised brand on the cover of a board paper, we are not that either, though we are frequently the delivery partner underneath one.

Straight answers

Questions before the first call

?
Where does the "median market salary" come from?
+
Live job-board postings and salary-survey data for each role in each city, refreshed quarterly. We take the median of the current advertised range rather than the average, because averages in this market are pulled upward by a small number of outliers. We will show you the source for any line on request.
?
What happens if market salaries fall?
+
Your rate falls at the next quarterly review. The formula runs in both directions, which is the point of publishing it. Note that contracted multi-year rates still carry an annual escalator of local CPI plus 2%, or 4%, whichever is greater — wage inflation in these markets is currently moderate and will not stay that way.
?
Can I get the 15% bundle margin on a small team?
+
No. At 15% margin on delivered cost the contract does not cover the cost of winning and running it unless it is spread across sixty or more people. We would rather decline than run your service at a loss, because a supplier losing money on your account is your problem before it is ours.
?
Why is a Malaysian agent more expensive than an Indian one?
+
Salaries are higher and the market is smaller. What you buy for the difference is a working day that matches Singapore, Hong Kong and Sydney, plus Bahasa, Mandarin and Tamil coverage. If your scope is asynchronous and English-only, India is the better answer and we will say so.
?
Do these rates apply to SAP, Oracle or cybersecurity roles?
+
No. Those are specialist roles priced on scarcity, not cost-plus, and they sit on a separate rate card at several times these levels. Asking for an S/4HANA architect at the L3 rate on this page would get you someone who is not an S/4HANA architect.
?
What is the smallest engagement you will take?
+
Four people on the Pilot tier for three months, or six people for a flexible BOT centre. Below that the setup and account cost outweighs the contract and neither of us benefits.
?
Can we mix delivery countries?
+
Yes, and for round-the-clock coverage you should. The Follow-the-Sun bundle runs South Africa for European hours and India for the rest, which gives genuine 24×7 without putting anyone on a permanent night shift. Two or more delivery countries on one contract counts toward the bundle tier.

What currency will you invoice in?
+
USD, GBP, ZAR, AED, MYR or INR. Where we quote outside the delivery country's currency, rates are fixed within a ±5% movement in the cross-rate.
