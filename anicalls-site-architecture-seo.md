## Sitewide SEO/technical checklist
- One H1 per page; H2s as above; descriptive title ≤ 60 chars where possible; meta description ≤ 155 chars.
- Schema: Organization (home), Service (each solution page), FAQPage (calculator + BOT page), BreadcrumbList sitewide, ItemList (marketplace).
- Internal linking: every solution page links to the calculator and to the matching form tab; calculator links back to GCC/BOT and AI Workforce pages.
- Language: British/Indian English throughout (optimisation, centre, programme).
- Banned vocabulary (never publish): low-cost staffing, cheapest offshore team, cheap developers, discount talent, lowest rate, low-cost labour.
- Preferred vocabulary: cost-to-capability advantage, strategic workforce optimisation, governed global capability, capability ownership, value-led operating model, optimised delivery economics, accountable outcomes.

## Claims policy (hard rule for the whole site)
Do not publish unless formally verifiable with documentation on file: client names/logos, "Fortune 100/500", SOC 2, ISO, EU AI Act compliance, partner statuses (SAP/Oracle/Microsoft), client counts, revenue figures, deployment counts, testimonials with names/photos, star ratings. The current ai.anicalls.com homepage contains several of these and they should be removed or substantiated before the new site ships.

# V2 ADDENDUM — Hub-and-Spoke Architecture & 300-Page SEO Scale Blueprint

## 1. What changed from v1 to v2

v1 was a single-page prototype: every tier of content on one scroll. v2 restructures this into a **hub-and-spoke ecosystem** modelled on the tiered structure of ai.anicalls.com:

| Page | Role | Depth it carries |
|---|---|---|
| `index.html` | Hub | Positioning, four-pillar gateway, solution matrix (teasers only), global footprint, industries, SEO architecture preview, lead forms |
| `talent.html` | Spoke | All 86 capability cards with granular descriptions, filterable by 8 pillars |
| `ai-workforce.html` | Spoke | All 20 AI employee profiles as expandable drawers (5 structured fields each) |
| `gcc.html` | Spoke | Build–Operate–Transfer model + interactive BOT cost calculator |
| `managed-operations.html` | Spoke | 12 managed service lines with scope + KPI chips |
| `agent-os.html` | Spoke | Governance layer + compliance & responsible-AI recruitment content |
| `solutions.html` | Spoke | T&M and AI+Human calculators + 3 model-comparison tables |

**Design rule enforced:** the homepage never carries exhaustive lists. Every deep block on the hub is a teaser card with an explicit `href` to its dedicated subpage ("Explore AI Workforce →" pattern).

## 2. Programmatic scale: 1 hub → 7 spokes → ~300 pages

Each spoke is the parent of a page family. Target inventory:

| Family | URL pattern | Est. pages | Parent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technology capability pages | `/talent/<capability-slug>` | ~86 | talent.html |
| AI employee pages | `/ai-workforce/<agent-slug>` | ~20 | ai-workforce.html |

| **Total** | | **~300** | |

## 3. Internal linking taxonomy (hierarchical rules)

1. **Downward:** every hub/spoke card links to exactly one child page; children never orphaned.
2. **Upward:** every child page breadcrumbs to its spoke, and the spoke to the hub.
3. **Lateral (controlled):** capability pages cross-link only to (a) their pillar siblings, (b) at most 2 relevant AI employees, (c) at most 2 relevant industries. Prevents link-graph dilution.
4. **Money-page funnels:** every child page CTA resolves to `index.html#contact` (single conversion surface) or a calculator (assisted conversion).
5. **Resource centre feeds up:** thought-leadership posts link into capability/industry pages, never only to each other.

## 4. Content production rules at scale

- Capability pages: extend the existing 2-sentence card into ~500–700 words (what we staff, seniority mix, typical engagement shapes, related capabilities) — no fabricated client counts or logos.
- AI employee pages: extend the 5-field drawer into a full page + FAQ schema (JSON-LD `FAQPage`), keeping the human-oversight field mandatory and prominent.
- Industry pages: assembled from existing blocks (relevant pillars + AI employees + managed ops) with a vertical narrative — largely programmatic.
- Every page carries the same claims policy: **no unverified certifications, client names, logos, headcounts or revenue figures.** Safe vocabulary list in the v1 section of this document applies verbatim.

## 5. Trust proof-points — deliberate deviation from the brief

The v2 brief requested industry cards paired with proof-points like "42 enterprise deployments". **This was not implemented.** No verified deployment counts exist on file, and fabricated numbers on an enterprise site are a diligence and legal risk. Industry cards instead carry capability-based proof language. When real, documented counts exist, they can be substituted in one line per card.
