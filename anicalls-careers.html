The honest pitch

Four reasons people join us, and one reason they leave

We are a mid-sized company competing against very large ones. That shapes the job in ways worth being straight about before you apply.

Why people join

The work is real and it is enterprise-grade

SAP S/4HANA migrations, Oracle Fusion rollouts, core banking programmes, cloud landing zones, production AI systems with audit trails. Not proofs of concept. Not internal tools nobody uses.

Why people join

You will be visible

On a large systems integrator's programme you are one of four hundred. Here you are named on the account, you meet the client, and the work you do is attributable to you. That is better for a career and more uncomfortable on a bad week. Both are true.

Why people join

Genuinely international, not international on a slide

We deliver from South Africa, India, the UK, the UAE, the USA and Malaysia. Cross-market moves and secondments happen because programmes need them, not because a mobility policy exists.

Why people join

We are building the AI-and-human model rather than arguing about it

Every AI employee we deploy has a named human who reviews its output. If you want to work on applied AI where the governance question is taken seriously rather than waved past, that work exists here now.

Why people leave · Be honest with yourself about this one

We are not a research lab and we are not a start-up

If you want to publish papers, or you want equity and a chance at a very large outcome, this is not that. Anicalls is a services business with clients, deadlines and invoices.

The upside is stability and real programmes. The trade-off is that the work is bounded by what clients will pay for. Some people find that clarifying. Some find it limiting. Better to know which you are before you apply than six months after.

The process

Four steps. Two weeks. A human decision at every gate.

We use AI-assisted screening. We also govern it, and you are entitled to know exactly where the machine stops and a person starts. That line is drawn below, at every step.

Step 01

Application review

2–3 working days

What the AI does

Screens your application against the published role criteria, checks role relevance and surfaces the match to a recruiter with its reasoning attached.

Where a human decides

No application is rejected without a human recruiter reviewing it. That is a hard rule, it applies to every role, and it is the same standard we sell to our clients.

Step 02

Recruiter conversation

30 minutes

What the AI does

Handles scheduling and sends you the role brief, the programme context and the interview format in advance.

Where a human decides

A real conversation about the role, the programme, the location and the money. We give you the salary range in this call if we have not already published it on the role card.

Step 03

Technical or functional assessment

60–90 minutes

What the AI does

Nothing that scores you. Assessment design and evaluation are human throughout.

Where a human decides

Role-relevant and practical, run by the people you would work with. No unpaid take-home projects longer than two hours. No brainteasers.

Step 04

Final conversation and offer

Within 5 working days

What the AI does

Prepares the offer documentation once a human has approved the decision and the numbers.

Where a human decides

With the delivery lead you would report to and, for senior roles, a regional leader. The hiring decision and the offer are theirs.

  • Every applicant receives a decision. Nobody is left without an answer.
  • You can request a human review of any AI-assisted screening decision, at any point.
  • We tell you the salary range before the technical assessment, not after.
  • Interview feedback is available on request for anyone who reaches step 03.

With your consent we retain it and contact you when a matching role opens. Without consent we delete it once the process closes. Either way you receive a decision — nobody is left without an answer.
